## What landed

- **Model**: `Claim.headline` (bool) + `Claim.role` (one of
  `DECOMPOSITION_ROLES` or null), strict-validated in the FR-1 error
  shape, serialised in `to_dict`, present in both JSON schemas.
- **Decomposition (`src/aee/decomposition.py`, new)**: `missing_roles`
  counts only sub-claims both present in the bundle AND linked via the
  parent's `depends_on`. A missing role produces a
  `decomposition_incomplete` failure (HIGH) and caps the parent's
  effective stage at `created` (R2.3). Weakest-link score propagation
  over `depends_on` is the existing mechanism and is unchanged.
- **Attribution (R4)**: the `attribution` role sub-claim is the
  attribution record — an ordinary claim with its own evidence,
  falsifier, stage, and status (R4.1/R4.2/R4.4). Contested state =
  author status `contradicted`, or an unresolved HIGH/CRITICAL
  failure in a contradiction category. A contested attribution caps
  the parent's effective stage at `checked` (R4.3); the cap record
  names the attribution claim's id.
- **Effective stages, one computation**: the engine computes
  decomposition/attribution overrides once per assessment and every
  surface reads them — `stage_caps`, `reported_statuses`, policy
  verdicts (judged on an effective-stage copy of the claim), the
  R1.4 outcome rule, and the summary (which now states decomposition
  and contested-attribution counts).
- **Priority (`src/aee/priority.py`, new; R7.1)**: `claim_priority` /
  `priority_index` read the earliest ledger entry whose assessment
  payload contains the claim id, returning sequence, timestamp,
  entry hash, and assessment digest. CLI: `aee priority --ledger
  <path> [--claim <id>]` (exit 2 for a never-registered claim).
- **Priority evidence (R7.2)**: `aee verify-evidence` resolves refs
  of the form `ledger:<path>#<sequence>`: the ledger must verify as
  a chain, the entry must exist, and a declared `content_hash` must
  equal the recomputed entry hash (recorded as
  `content_hash_status: verified`). New finding categories:
  `ledger_ref_invalid`, `ledger_entry_missing`, `ledger_chain_invalid`.
- **R7.3 audit**: (a) freshness reads only `observed_at` — pinned by
  a regression test (identical claims at different stages score
  identically, notes included); (b) no mechanism penalises a
  priority citation — the shared-evidence warning is reporting-only
  and cross-claim citations are not deduped away; (c) no policy gate
  references disclosure timing. No defect found; nothing filed.

## Honest limits

- Decomposition is structural (R2.4): the engine cannot tell whether
  a sub-claim's text actually discharges its role.
- The attribution contest test reads the attribution claim's failure
  list as regenerated by the stress tester at assessment time; a
  contest expressed only in prose with no contradicting evidence and
  no contradicted status is invisible to it — by design (R4.2
  requires the dispute to be registered through evidence/status).
